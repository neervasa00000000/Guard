# Alternative font-build sensitivity

This new run defines the previously unspecified 36 cells as six fonts × three sizes × A1/A2, on exact clean surname layout boxes and the original seeded 2000 targets per font-size. Both sides use their own matched renderer; A2 respects the finite uniform pen support. Builds and thresholds were frozen in protocol.json before scoring.

2 of 36 cells shift by at least 4 percentage points. This does not verify the historical “2 of 36, 4–9 points” claim because the original alternative binaries and cell definition were not archived. No builds were chosen after observing scores. The explicit modern sensitivity experiment and its paired target-bootstrap CIs are fully recorded; the historical claim remains marked unverified. Font binaries and target strings are not redistributed.
