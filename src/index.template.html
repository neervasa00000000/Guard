      About TileSeal

      TileSeal (repository: Guard)

      Black out parts of a picture, seal it as a .GRD, and check later that the visible pixels match the seal. This site creates self-signed files. Verifying a signer's identity requires a trusted key configured in the verifier.

      The editor rounds boxes outward at 16 px by default. Padding widens them in steps of 128 px to reduce clues about hidden text length. Box shape can still reveal information. The editor offers 16, 32 and 64 px rounding. The Python CLI also offers 8 px for compatible seals.

      Download plain picture saves a PNG. Keep the .GRD to check its proof again.

      IEEE Access manuscript in preparation. The manuscript cites f8f2558, which predates the browser padding control.
